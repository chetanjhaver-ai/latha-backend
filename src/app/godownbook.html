Missing Bills

Bill numbers within a series that fall between the lowest and highest received, but haven't come in yet. Updates automatically as bills are added or removed.

Admins can dismiss a number permanently (e.g. a cancelled/voided bill) — dismissed numbers move to the Ignored list and can be restored from there.
